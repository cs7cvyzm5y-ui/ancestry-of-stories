# Research expansion plan after Version 0 validation

Version 0 testing indicates that the interaction model is useful enough to expand, but several clusters remain research-thin. Expansion should proceed by **author/work dossiers**, not by indiscriminately adding famous names.

## Immediate research dossiers

### Ernest Hemingway
Build author formation: birth/death/active dates, journalism, World War I, expatriate Paris, mentors and peers, documented reading, bullfighting/Spain, Spanish Civil War, stylistic theory, major works, and downstream influence. Avoid converting biography into psychological causation without evidence.

### Edgar Allan Poe
Build ancestry as well as descendants: Gothic and Romantic sources, documented reading, magazine culture, literary theories, detective-story formation, horror/weird-fiction transmission, and downstream genre branches.

### Gabriel García Márquez / One Hundred Years of Solitude
Expand Colombian and Caribbean context: family oral storytelling, Aracataca, journalism, Liberal–Conservative civil wars, banana economy and 1928 massacre, colonial/neocolonial history, Latin American Boom, and documented literary sources. Keep historical events distinct from textual influence.

### Chinua Achebe / Things Fall Apart
Expand Igbo oral/language tradition, family and Christian missionary upbringing, colonial education, British colonial expansion, Yeats title source, African representation/counter-canon concerns, publishing history, and downstream African literature.

### C. S. Lewis / Tolkien / Inklings
Represent mutual exchange rather than a one-way influence arrow: myth and faith discussions, works-in-progress criticism, Tolkien's role in Lewis's conversion, Lewis's encouragement of Tolkien, and their divergent approaches to fantasy and theology.

### Dungeon Crawler Carl
Verify direct textual references separately from inferred influence. In particular, research the text's explicit analogies to reality television / *Survivor* / *The Running Man*, horror reading, death-game fiction, games, and audience-participation structure. Do not upgrade a reference into creative influence without author evidence.

## Historical-context rule

Historical context should be useful for reader education as well as causal analysis. Each context node should explain:
- what happened;
- where and when;
- why it matters to the selected author/work;
- whether the relationship is direct subject matter, documented lived experience, or broader environment.

The future Timeline view should make elapsed time and overlapping lifetimes/periods visible.
